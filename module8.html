Module 08: Create Video Conference using Team

Learning Outcomes: After completion of this learning module, learners will be able to log in to MS Teams, schedule and manage video conferences, configure security and lobby options, manage participant permissions, share screens and files, record meetings, and properly end conferences[cite: 7].

Learning Unit 1: Schedule a Meeting

  • Logging In: Open Microsoft Teams app or go to teams.microsoft.com, enter your work/school credentials, and complete Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) if enabled[cite: 7].
  • Interface Navigation: Key sidebar sections include Activity, Chat, Teams, Calendar, Calls, and Files[cite: 7].
  • Calendar View & New Meeting: Click the Calendar icon, then click + New meeting or select a specific time slot on the grid[cite: 7].
  • Meeting Details: Provide a descriptive title, add attendees (names or emails), set start/end date and time, duration, and location[cite: 7].
  • Meeting Options: Configure recurrence rules, agendas, meeting notes, and lobby/presenter access controls[cite: 7].

Learning Unit 2: Send Meeting Invitation

  • Customizing Options: Control who can bypass the lobby, mute attendees upon entry, restrict presenters, and manage chat/reaction permissions[cite: 7].
  • Adding Participants: Type participant names (internal) or email addresses (external guests) into the required attendees field[cite: 7].
  • Automatic Invocation: Saving a meeting automatically sends email invitations containing the meeting link, date, time, and agenda[cite: 7].

Learning Unit 3: Join and Manage the Video Conference (Participant Side)

  • Joining Methods: Join via calendar link, meeting ID and passcode, channel/chat windows, or web browsers without installing the Teams app[cite: 7].
  • Pre-join Settings: Configure audio and video devices, and toggle your camera and microphone on or off before entering[cite: 7].
  • In-Meeting Controls: Manage video presence, mute/unmute your microphone (shortcut: Ctrl + Spacebar), share desktop or application windows, and use chat for professional communication[cite: 7].

Learning Unit 4: Manage Participants (Admin / Organizer Side)

  • Lobby Settings: Restrict meeting access (e.g., Only organizers, People in my org, Everyone) and admit or deny waiting participants[cite: 7].
  • Participant Controls: Mute/unmute participants individually or click Mute all to reduce background noise[cite: 7]. Remove disruptive participants if necessary[cite: 7].
  • Sharing Permissions: Control screen sharing rights (Everyone, People in my organization, Specific people, Only me) and grant or revoke remote control[cite: 7].
  • Recording: Start and stop recording via More actions (...) > Start recording[cite: 7].

Learning Unit 5: Manage Meeting Ending

  • Accessing Recordings: Channel meeting recordings are saved in the Files > Recordings folder of the channel's SharePoint site. Non-channel recordings are saved in the organizer's OneDrive Recordings folder[cite: 7].
  • Sharing Resources: Automatically posted recording links, shared files, downloaded chat transcripts, and collaborative meeting notes[cite: 7].
  • Ending the Meeting: Organizers can select More actions (...) > End meeting to disconnect all participants simultaneously[cite: 7].
