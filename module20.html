Module 20: How-to Articles – Understanding of the Search Engines

Learning Outcomes: After completing this module, learners will be able to search and evaluate "how-to" topics, utilize reliable research platforms (Google Scholar, YouTube), differentiate between primary/secondary sources, structure and write effective how-to articles, integrate visual aids (images and tables) with proper citations, review for clarity and factual correctness, and publish content on appropriate platforms[cite: 7].

1. How Search Engines Work & Core SEO Elements

  • The Three Stages: Search engines operate via Crawling (bots discovering content), Indexing (storing data in massive databases), and Ranking (sorting results using algorithms based on relevance and authority)[cite: 7].
  • Essential SEO Factors: Focus on targeted keywords, optimized meta tags (title under 60 characters, description under 160 characters), mobile-friendliness, fast page speed, and quality backlinks[cite: 7].

2. Researching & Evaluating Sources

  • Google Scholar & Video Tutorials: Use academic engines for peer-reviewed citations and targeted video queries (including year and skill level) for visual walkthroughs[cite: 7].
  • Evaluating Credibility: Assess author background, publication dates, and Domain Authority (.gov, .edu, and .org domains vs. user-generated forums)[cite: 7].

3. Structuring & Writing How-to Articles

  • Article Structure: Begin with an actionable H1 title (e.g., "How to..."), a concise introduction outlining the problem, numbered step-by-step instructions, expert tips, common mistakes to avoid, and a strong conclusion[cite: 7].
  • Data & Information Handling: Extract core facts, condense lengthy information effectively, link data smoothly to arguments, and maintain a consistent, professional tone[cite: 7].

4. Adding Visual Aids & Tables

  • Images (HTML): Embed images using the <img src="path" alt="description"> tag and properly cite original creators (APA or Harvard style)[cite: 7].
  • Data Tables: Organize complex data in grids using <table>, <tr>, <th>, and <td> tags, ensuring proper alignment (left-align text, right-align numbers) and adding source credit lines below[cite: 7].

5. Reviewing, Finalizing & Publishing

  • Content Quality: Check for factual correctness, ensure clear flow and coherence, and proofread minor grammar and formatting details[cite: 7].
  • Publishing Platforms: Choose the ideal platform based on audience goals, such as Content Management Systems (WordPress, Medium), personal blogs, or academic databases[cite: 7].
