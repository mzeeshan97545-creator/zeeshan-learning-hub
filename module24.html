Module 24: Maintain Internet Safety

Learning Outcomes: After completing this module, learners will understand cybersecurity threats and risks, apply safe browsing and shopping practices, recognize phishing attempts, implement strong password management and 2FA, adhere to data privacy regulations (like GDPR), secure mobile devices, and maintain legal and ethical standards online[cite: 7].

1. Cybersecurity Awareness & Threats

  • Common Cyber Threats: Includes malware (viruses, ransomware, spyware), phishing and social engineering, DDoS attacks, Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attacks, and AI-driven cyber threats[cite: 7].
  • Incident Reporting: Promptly report suspicious activities, security breaches, or cyberbullying to IT teams or relevant authorities to minimize damage[cite: 7].

2. Safe Browsing & Website Security

  • Identifying Secure Sites: Look for HTTPS:// protocols, valid SSL/TLS certificates, and browser padlock icons[cite: 7].
  • Avoiding Suspicious Sites: Beware of URL anomalies (misspellings/typosquatting), poor grammar, fake prizes, and unsolicited pop-up downloads[cite: 7].
  • Network Safety: Avoid sensitive transactions over public Wi-Fi; use trusted VPNs and keep operating systems and antivirus tools updated[cite: 7].

3. Password Security & Authentication

  • Strong Passwords: Create passphrases or passwords at least 12–16 characters long combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols[cite: 7].
  • Password Managers & 2FA: Use password managers to store unique credentials securely and enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA/2FA) on all accounts[cite: 7].

4. Data Privacy, Social Media & Communication Safety

  • Privacy Principles: Adhere to data minimization, purpose limitation, transparency, and accuracy under regulations like GDPR[cite: 7].
  • Social Media Habits: Limit oversharing of personal information, adjust privacy settings, and be vigilant against social engineering and deepfake scams[cite: 7].
  • Secure Messaging: Use end-to-end encrypted communication apps and verify participants to protect sensitive conversations[cite: 7].

5. Online Shopping, Mobile Security & Legal Compliance

  • Financial Safety: Evaluate online sellers through customer reviews and secure payment methods (credit cards, digital wallets with buyer protection)[cite: 7].
  • Mobile Device Security: Secure devices with biometrics/passcodes, application permissions control, regular patch updates, and remote wipe configurations[cite: 7].
  • Legal & Ethical Standards: Respect copyrights, avoid plagiarism, refrain from cyberharassment, and uphold ethical responsibility in digital spaces[cite: 7].
